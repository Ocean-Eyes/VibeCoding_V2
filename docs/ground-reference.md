# Groundwork references

- Ude-garami: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AIlTvZb4RlE (Kodokan / IJF Academy).
  Viewed frames at 4, 8, 13, 20, 30, and 40 seconds. Wrist capture and the
  second hand connecting beneath the arm inform the exercise.
  The requested right-hand wrist grip and left-hand figure-four connection
  are an authored solo adaptation; the opponent's right elbow remains at 90
  degrees with the forearm raised upward, as requested.
  The reference arms use a mounted shoulder frame turned slightly toward the
  right arm, with a rigid 0.41 m shoulder width. The right hand approaches the
  wrist from above; the left waits outside the elbow, passes underneath, then
  connects to the right forearm. Fingers close after the palm reaches its grip.
  The user's diagram specifies the two palm contacts and crossing order. A
  third stage raises the connected left elbow; uke's elbow rotates slightly
  around the fixed shoulder-to-wrist axis without changing bone lengths.
  Camera practice checks a left-elbow rise after the connection, not torque.
  `scripts/bake-ground-contact.mjs` selects continuous elbow paths against the
  same upper-arm, forearm, palm and finger meshes drawn by `throw-render.js`.
  Both bone lengths remain fixed during playback and interpolation. The demo
  releases the grip by reversing the same path instead of jumping to its start.
  This is hand-position practice, not a measurement of joint torque or actual contact.
- Kesa-gatame: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NDaQuJOFBYk (Kodokan / IJF Academy).
  Viewed frames at 4, 8, 13, 20, 30, and 40 seconds. Right-side seated position,
  split legs, head wrap and captured near arm inform the three stages.
  The camera checks a seated pose and arm positions, not holding pressure.

The 3D models are original instructional approximations, not motion capture or
official Kodokan models. Kesa practice uses an unmodified landscape camera view
with a fixed 2D reference in its lower-right corner. Ude practice shows the lying
opponent and colored reference arms beside the unmodified camera image.
